## Rejected inheritance

- Do not inherit strong claims such as `proves`, `ensures`, `readiness for embedded deployment`, or `significantly` without matching evidence.
- Do not inherit Engineering-AI's hardware, latency, uncertainty, confidence-interval, or statistical detail unless the MS-AgentNet manuscript contains corresponding experiments.
- Do not inherit JESSOHRUL's RUL, multi-task, CEEMDAN, or joint-assessment language where it does not apply.
- Do not reproduce the reference papers' repeated numerical summaries or revision-stage defensive explanations.
